# 💫 About Me:
I’m Dr. Swati Hurmade, a clinician-turned-health informatician currently pursuing my Master of Science in Health Informatics at Indiana University Indianapolis (GPA: 3.96). My academic and professional journey bridges healthcare, data science, and digital transformation, combining a clinical background in dentistry with technical expertise in data analytics, interoperability, and health information systems.<br>As a Health Informatics Intern at Wellness Connection Inc, I designed and deployed a scalable Community Wellness Dashboard in DHIS2, transforming multi-year PHQ-9 depression screening data into actionable visual insights for population health management. My experience spans data engineering, predictive analytics, and CDSS development, where I’ve applied statistical modeling, RAG-based pipelines, and geospatial mapping to uncover clinical patterns and support decision-making.<br>I’m passionate about advancing evidence-based, technology-enabled healthcare, building interoperable solutions that enhance clinical outcomes, support preventive care, and empower both patients and providers.
